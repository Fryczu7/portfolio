04

Editing

A raw recording becomes a ready Premiere project with layers: cuts, b-roll, zooms, UI animations and effects. I open it and adjust by hand only what I want.

66ready-made animation blocks: captions, bubbles, cards, maps

  • Remotion
  • HyperFrames
05

Captions

Word by word, synced to the voice, broken by meaning. I fix a typo in the text, not frame by frame.

1word on screen at a time, synced to the voice

Retention

A bot reads from TikTok Studio how many viewers stay after 3 seconds and how many watch to the end, then maps it onto the sentences of the recording. I know which sentence killed the video.
